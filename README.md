## 👋 About

I'm a **Data Scientist** based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. I take a problem from raw data to something people can use: cleaning and exploring the data, training the model, then wrapping it in an app or pipeline.

Lately my work is agentic: LangGraph systems that retrieve, grade their own answers and correct course, plus a voice assistant that works in Urdu and English.

- 🔮 **Predictive ML:** churn prediction and recommendation systems, from SQL and EDA to a deployed app
- 🤖 **LLM and RAG agents:** hybrid search (dense + BM25), cross-encoder reranking, self-correcting retrieval
- ⚙️ **Apps and automation:** Streamlit, Flask, Laravel and FastAPI apps, plus Selenium and Pandas data pipelines

**Right now**
- 💼 Data Scientist at **Optimum Tech** (since Jul 2026)
- 🧑‍💻 Freelance AI and backend developer (since Feb 2026)
- 🎓 BS Computer Science, University of Agriculture, Faisalabad (2024 to 2028)
